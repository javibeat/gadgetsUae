Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

GadgetsUAE (gadgetsdxb.com) is a static website. There are no user accounts, no checkout and no forms that send your personal data to us. We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

What is stored in your browser

Your favourites list is kept in your own browser using local storage. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and disappears if you clear your browser data.

Visitor statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views, searches on this site, favourites and clicks through to Amazon.ae. It sets no cookies, does not build a profile of you and only reports anonymous, aggregated numbers (for example, which pages and products are popular and which country visits come from).

Amazon affiliate links

Product links point to Amazon.ae and include our Amazon Associates tag. When you follow one, Amazon may set cookies to attribute a purchase to GadgetsUAE. Anything you do on Amazon.ae, including what you view or buy, is governed by Amazon's privacy notice, not ours. We never see your name, address or payment details.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Those providers may log standard technical data such as your IP address to serve the files. Apart from the Umami statistics described above, we do not run advertising networks or tracking pixels.

Newsletter

If you enter your email in the newsletter box, it is used only to send occasional GadgetsUAE updates. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email, or by writing to us.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please contact us.
