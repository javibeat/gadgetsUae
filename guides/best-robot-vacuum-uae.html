Frequently asked questions

Do robot vacuums work on marble and porcelain tile?
Yes, they are at their best on hard floors. Choose a model with a mop for the fine dust film that settles daily.
How often should the dust bag be changed?
With an auto-empty station, roughly every six to eight weeks for a two-bedroom apartment.
Do they map multiple floors for villas?
All three robots here store several maps, so you can carry the robot between floors.
