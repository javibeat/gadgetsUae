Frequently asked questions

When is the Dubai Shopping Festival 2026–27?
DSF 2026–27 runs from 4 December 2026 to 17 January 2027. It is the 32nd edition and, at 45 days, the longest so far. Check visitdubai.com for the official programme.
Are there online deals during DSF?
Most DSF offers happen in Dubai malls and stores, and some retailers run matching online offers. Prices on Amazon.ae change all the time, so the easiest check is to open the product on Amazon.ae before paying in a store.
Is DSF a good time to buy a new iPhone?
New iPhones rarely get big discounts. During DSF the savings usually come from bank instalment plans, cashback offers and trade-in credit, so compare the total cost of each offer, not only the price.
Do products bought in Dubai have a UAE warranty?
Buy from the official brand store or an authorised seller and check that the box or listing says UAE or Middle East version. That version is covered by the local warranty.
What tech makes a good gift during DSF?
Safe gifts are AirPods, a Kindle, an AirTag pack or a power bank. For a bigger gift, a Nintendo Switch 2 or a PS5 Pro, especially now that GTA VI is out.
