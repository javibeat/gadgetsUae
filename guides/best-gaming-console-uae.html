Frequently asked questions

Are games more expensive in the UAE?
Physical games on Amazon.ae are usually priced in line with Europe. Digital stores use regional pricing; the PlayStation and Xbox UAE stores are competitive.
Do I need the UAE version of the console?
For warranty and the correct plug, yes. Games themselves are region-free on all current consoles.
Is Game Pass available in the UAE?
Yes, Game Pass Ultimate is available in the UAE with cloud gaming, which makes the Xbox Series S an excellent value buy.
