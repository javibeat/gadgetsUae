Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026 in the UAE?
Black Friday is on Friday 27 November 2026 and Cyber Monday on 30 November 2026. Amazon.ae calls its event White Friday; last year it ran from 20 to 30 November, and the 2026 sale is expected to follow a similar window. Check Amazon.ae for the official dates.
Is White Friday the same as Black Friday?
Yes. White Friday is the name Amazon.ae and some Gulf retailers use for the Black Friday sale. Noon calls it Yellow Friday. The deals and timing are the same idea: the last week or so of November.
Will the iPhone 18 Pro Max be cheaper on Black Friday?
Brand-new iPhones are rarely discounted much. The value usually comes from bank offers, instalments, trade-in credit and bundles. Older models such as the iPhone 17 and iPhone 16 series see bigger price cuts.
Is GTA 6 on sale for Black Friday?
GTA VI launches on 19 November 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Brand-new games are rarely discounted at launch, but consoles and controllers often are, so it is a good moment to buy the hardware.
Do I need Amazon Prime for White Friday deals?
Not to buy, but Prime members usually get early access to deals and free fast delivery across the UAE. Amazon.ae offers a free Prime trial, so you can sign up just before the sale.
How do I know if a White Friday deal is real?
Check that the seller is Amazon.ae or the official brand store, that the listing says UAE or Middle East version for warranty, and compare the price with the past few weeks rather than with the crossed-out list price.
