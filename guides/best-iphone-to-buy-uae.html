Which iPhone to Buy in UAE (2026)

Apple sells more iPhones than ever in the UAE, and since September 2026 the line-up includes the iPhone 18 Pro, the 18 Pro Max and Apple's first foldable, the iPhone Duo. The standard iPhone 18 is not out until spring 2027, so last year's models are still very much in the picture. Here is which one to buy.

Quick answerBest overall: iPhone 18 Pro Max. Smaller pro: iPhone 18 Pro. Want a foldable: iPhone Duo. Best value: iPhone 17. Cheapest: iPhone 16e.
01

Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max (UAE Version)

The best iPhone you can buy: A20 Pro, three 48 MP cameras and the biggest battery. If you keep phones for three or four years, this is the one.

02

Apple iPhone 18 Pro (UAE Version)

All the Pro Max cameras and performance in a smaller body that fits one hand. The pick if big phones annoy you.

03

Apple iPhone Duo Foldable Smartphone

Apple's first foldable opens into a 7.6-inch screen with Apple Pencil support. Spectacular, but the most expensive iPhone ever, so buy it for the screen, not the camera.

04

Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max 256GB

Last year's Pro Max is still superb, and it is the model most likely to see real price cuts during White Friday.

05

Apple iPhone 17 256GB

The best value iPhone right now: 120 Hz screen, great battery and the newest standard model until the iPhone 18 arrives in 2027.

06

Apple iPhone Air 256GB

The thinnest iPhone. A style choice with one camera and a smaller battery, best for light users.

07

Apple iPhone 16e 128GB

The cheapest way into a current iPhone with Apple Intelligence. Ideal for teenagers, parents or a second phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is the iPhone 18 Pro Max worth it over the iPhone 17 Pro Max?
If you are coming from an iPhone 15 or older, yes: the A20 Pro and the camera upgrades are noticeable. From a 17 Pro Max the jump is small, so keep your phone or wait for a deal.
When does the standard iPhone 18 come out?
Apple only launched the Pro models and the foldable iPhone Duo in September 2026. The standard iPhone 18 is expected in spring 2027, alongside a new iPhone 18e.
Are iPhones on Amazon.ae the UAE version?
iPhones sold by Amazon.ae are Middle East versions with Apple warranty, which Apple honours at its UAE stores.
Is the iPhone Duo available in the UAE?
Yes. The iPhone Duo went on sale in the UAE on 23 October 2026 in star white and night sky.
