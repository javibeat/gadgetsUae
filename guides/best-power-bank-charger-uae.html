Frequently asked questions

What is the power bank limit on Emirates and Etihad?
Up to 100 Wh in hand luggage without approval, never in checked bags. All power banks in this guide qualify.
Is GaN better than a normal charger?
GaN chargers are smaller and cooler for the same wattage, which matters in summer and when travelling.
Will a 100 W charger damage my phone?
No. USB-C Power Delivery negotiates the safe wattage with the device, so a 100 W charger is fine for earbuds or a phone.
