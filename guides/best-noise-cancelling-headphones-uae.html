Frequently asked questions

Over-ear or earbuds for flying?
Over-ear wins for comfort and passive isolation on long-haul; earbuds win for pocketability. Many frequent flyers own both.
Can I use them wired on the plane?
Yes, every pair here includes a 3.5 mm cable or works with a USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter, so seat-back screens are covered.
How long do the batteries last?
Between 30 and 70 hours depending on the model, so a full Dubai to Los Angeles round trip on one charge is realistic.
