Frequently asked questions

Do laptops on Amazon.ae come with an Arabic keyboard?
Most are English-Arabic bilingual keyboards. The listing states the layout; English-only versions are also sold.
Is the MacBook warranty international?
Apple provides a one-year worldwide warranty, and the Apple Stores in Dubai and Abu Dhabi handle service locally.
How much RAM do I need in 2026?
16 GB is the sensible minimum for a laptop you will keep for four years. Go to 24 GB or more for video editing and heavy multitasking.
