Best Smartwatches in UAE (2026)

A watch is the gadget most tied to your phone, so the first question is iPhone or Android. The second is whether you train outdoors in the heat, where battery life and GPS accuracy separate the toys from the tools.

Quick answeriPhone: Apple Watch Series 11 (or SE 3 on a budget). Samsung: Galaxy Watch8. Serious sport: Garmin Forerunner 570. Under AED 200: Xiaomi Smart Band 10.
01

Apple Watch Series 11 GPS 46mm Aluminium

The best all-round watch for iPhone owners, with the most polished health features and UAE payments.

02

Apple Watch SE 3 GPS 44mm Aluminium

Most of the Series 11 experience for far less. The pick for a first Apple Watch or for kids.

03

Apple Watch Ultra 3 GPS + Cellular 49mm Titanium

Multi-day battery, a brighter screen for desert sun and dive-grade toughness.

04

Samsung Galaxy Watch8 Bluetooth 44mm

The natural partner for a Galaxy phone: sleep coaching, ECG and a slimmer body this year.

05

Garmin Forerunner 570 47mm GPS Running Smartwatch

The runner’s pick: two weeks of battery, dual-band GPS and training load that actually helps.

06

Garmin fenix 8 47mm AMOLED Multisport GPS Watch

For triathletes and hikers who want a watch that lasts a month and survives anything.

07

Huawei Watch GT 6 46mm Smartwatch

Two weeks of battery with a classic look, and it works with both iPhone and Android.

08

Xiaomi Smart Band 10 Fitness Tracker

Steps, sleep, heart rate and notifications for the price of a lunch. Hard to argue with.

Frequently asked questions

Does Apple Pay or Samsung Pay work in the UAE?
Yes, both work with most UAE banks, so you can tap the watch on the metro gates and in shops.
Which watch has the longest battery?
Garmin fenix 8 and Huawei Watch GT 6 last two weeks or more. Apple and Samsung watches need charging every one to two days.
Can I get cellular models in the UAE?
Yes, Etisalat by e& and du both support eSIM on Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch cellular models.
