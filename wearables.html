Frequently asked questions

Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch in the UAE?
Match the watch to your phone. Apple Watch only works with iPhone; Galaxy Watch works best with Samsung and other Android phones. Both support UAE payments and local carriers for cellular models.
Which watch is best for running in UAE heat?
Garmin Forerunner 570 and fenix 9 Pro have the longest battery and the most accurate GPS for outdoor training. Apple Watch Ultra 4 is the pick if you want an iPhone companion that also does serious sport.
Do smartwatches handle sweat and pool swimming?
All the watches listed here are rated to at least 5 ATM, so pool swimming and sweat are fine. Rinse them after sea water.
