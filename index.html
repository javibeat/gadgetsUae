Frequently asked questions

Does Amazon.ae deliver to all UAE cities?
Yes. Amazon.ae delivers across the UAE, with same-day or next-day Prime delivery in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman for most products. Free shipping applies on eligible orders over AED 100.
Why don't you show prices?
Amazon prices change several times a day and only Amazon is allowed to publish them. Instead of showing a number that may already be wrong, every card takes you to the live Amazon.ae page where the price, stock and delivery date are current.
What is the return policy for electronics in UAE?
Amazon.ae offers a 15-day return window on most electronics, provided the product is in its original packaging with all accessories.
Are products on Amazon.ae authentic?
Products sold by Amazon.ae or its authorised sellers are genuine and covered by the manufacturer warranty and Amazon's A-to-Z guarantee. Check the "Sold by" line on the listing.
How does GadgetsUAE choose products?
We hand-pick every product for the UAE market and rotate which ones are featured each day. We earn a commission from Amazon when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you.
