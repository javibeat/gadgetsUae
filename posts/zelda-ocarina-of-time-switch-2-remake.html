Zelda: Ocarina of Time on Switch 2: is the remake worth it?

Nintendo rebuilt one of the most loved games ever for Switch 2. Here is what changed and who should buy it.

Quick answerYes. The Switch 2 remake (out 5 November 2026) is one of the best-reviewed games of the year, with modern controls, full voice acting and a steady 60fps. It only runs on Nintendo Switch 2.

What is new in the remake?

This is a full remake, not a remaster. Reviewers highlight:

  • Fully voiced dialogue and cutscenes, something the original never had.
  • A rebuilt look with a fully 3D Castle Town and an orchestral soundtrack.
  • 60fps both on the TV and in handheld mode.
  • Modern controls borrowed from Breath of the Wild: a jump button, free camera, item wheel and D-pad shortcuts.

What do the critics say?

Launch reviews put it among the highest-rated games of 2026, around 96 on Metacritic at the time of writing. The few complaints: it plays it safe, some puzzles feel easier than before, and the new art style divides fans of the original.

What do you need to play it?

The remake is a Switch 2 exclusive, so it does not run on the original Switch.

  • The console: Nintendo Switch 2 (UAE version).
  • For long sessions on the TV: the Switch 2 Pro Controller.
  • For extra space: Switch 2 only reads microSD Express cards, like the SanDisk microSD Express 256GB.

Should you buy it?

If you never played the original, this is the easiest way to see why people still call it one of the greatest games ever made. If you did, the new controls and voice acting make it feel fresh enough for a second trip to Hyrule. And if a Switch 2 is on your White Friday list, this is the game to put next to it.
