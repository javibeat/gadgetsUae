GTA 6 in the UAE: release time, download size and what to play it on

Everything a UAE player needs before 19 November: when it unlocks, how much space to free up, and which hardware is worth it.

Quick answerGTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and is expected to unlock at midnight UAE time. Free up about 250GB. The PS5 Pro on a 4K TV is the best way to play; there is no PC version at launch.

When does GTA 6 unlock in the UAE?

Grand Theft Auto VI releases on Thursday 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar uses staggered regional unlocks, and the UAE slot is reported as midnight GST (8pm UTC on 18 November). There is no PC version at launch.

Pre-loading is already open on PS5, so you can download the game now and play the minute it unlocks. On Xbox, the pre-load opens at a single moment worldwide.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Rockstar asks players to allow up to 212GB of free space, depending on the console. Day-one patches add more, so plan on roughly 250GB free. That is a problem for many consoles:

  • PS5 Slim (1TB): fits, but you will probably need to delete a couple of big games.
  • Xbox Series S (512GB): GTA 6 alone takes almost half the usable space.
  • Any PS5: you can add an M.2 SSD with a heatsink, such as the Samsung 990 PRO 2TB, which slots inside the console in a few minutes.

PS5, PS5 Pro or Xbox: which is best for GTA 6?

PlayStation 5 Pro is the pick if you have a 4K TV. Rockstar has not detailed PS5 Pro enhancements yet, and early file analysis that hints at extras is unconfirmed, but the Pro is the most powerful console the game runs on.

PS5 Slim with disc drive is the value choice, and the disc drive lets you buy a boxed copy and resell it later.

Xbox Series X is the right buy if your friends play on Xbox. The game is the same on both families of consoles.

Frequently asked questions

What time does GTA 6 come out in the UAE?

GTA 6 is expected to unlock at midnight UAE time (GST) on 19 November 2026, which is 8pm UTC on 18 November. Pre-loading is already open on PS5.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. At launch GTA 6 is only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC date.

How much storage does GTA 6 need?

Rockstar says to allow up to 212GB depending on the console. With day-one updates, keep about 250GB free.

Does GTA 6 run on Xbox Series S?

Yes, GTA 6 is released on both Xbox Series X and Series S, but on the 512GB Series S it takes almost half of the storage.
