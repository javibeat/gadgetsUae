iPhone 18 Pro Max vs iPhone 17 Pro Max: should you upgrade?

The new Pro Max improves the camera and the battery. Here is who should upgrade and who should grab last year’s model instead.

Quick answerComing from an iPhone 15 or older: get the iPhone 18 Pro Max. Coming from a 16 Pro or 17 Pro Max: keep your phone. On a tighter budget, the iPhone 17 Pro Max is the one most likely to drop during White Friday.

The key differences

  • Camera: the 18 Pro Max main camera has a variable aperture (from f/1.48 to f/4.0) that you can also set manually. The 17 Pro Max uses a fixed f/1.78 lens. In practice: better background blur control and sharper group shots.
  • Battery: up to 45 hours of video playback on the 18 Pro Max versus up to 39 hours on the 17 Pro Max.
  • Chip: A20 Pro versus A19 Pro. Both are far faster than anything most people need.
  • Charging: faster wired charging on the 18 Pro Max (around 50% in 15 minutes with a 60W or stronger adapter, according to Apple).
  • Video: Cinematic mode goes up to 4K at 60fps, up from 4K30.
  • Design: same 6.9-inch size, a smaller Dynamic Island.

Who should buy the iPhone 18 Pro Max?

Anyone coming from an iPhone 15 or older, anyone who takes a lot of photos of people and food, and anyone who keeps a phone for four years: the bigger battery is the upgrade you will feel every day in the UAE heat, when phones work harder.

Who should buy the iPhone 17 Pro Max instead?

If you want a big Pro iPhone but not the newest price, last year’s model is still excellent, with the same screen size and three 48MP cameras. It is also the model most likely to see real price cuts during White Friday.

And the foldable iPhone Duo?

The iPhone Duo opens into a 7.6-inch screen with Apple Pencil support. Buy it for the big screen, not the camera: the 18 Pro Max is still the better camera phone.

Don’t forget the charger

iPhones ship without a charger. To get the fastest charging on the 18 Pro Max you need a 60W or stronger USB-C adapter, such as the Anker Prime 100W, which also charges a MacBook.

Frequently asked questions

Is the iPhone 18 Pro Max worth upgrading from the 17 Pro Max?

For most people, no. The camera gets a variable aperture and the battery lasts longer, but the 17 Pro Max is still excellent. The upgrade makes sense from an iPhone 15 or older.

What is the main camera difference?

The iPhone 18 Pro Max main camera has a variable aperture from f/1.48 to f/4.0. The iPhone 17 Pro Max has a fixed f/1.78 aperture.

Which has the better battery?

The iPhone 18 Pro Max, rated for up to 45 hours of video playback versus up to 39 hours on the iPhone 17 Pro Max.

When did the iPhone 18 Pro Max launch in the UAE?

The iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max went on sale in the UAE on 18 September 2026.
