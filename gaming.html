Frequently asked questions

Which gaming console is best to buy in UAE?
It depends on how you play. The Nintendo Switch 2 is the family and portable pick, the PlayStation 5 has the strongest exclusives, and the Xbox Series X is the best value with Game Pass. All are sold on Amazon.ae with Prime delivery.
Can I use international gaming consoles in UAE?
Yes, modern consoles are region-free for games. For warranty and the correct power plug we still recommend the UAE version sold by Amazon.ae.
When does GTA 6 come out in the UAE?
Grand Theft Auto VI launches on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, the same day worldwide. There is no PC version at launch. Physical copies are expected on Amazon.ae and at major UAE retailers.
Is the new Zelda: Ocarina of Time out on Switch 2?
Yes. The Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time came out on 5 November 2026. It is a Switch 2 exclusive, so it does not run on the original Switch.
Is a handheld like Steam Deck or ROG Ally worth it in the UAE?
If you travel or commute a lot, yes. Both run PC games natively and pair with a monitor at home. The Steam Deck OLED is simpler; the ROG Xbox Ally X20 is more powerful and runs Windows.
