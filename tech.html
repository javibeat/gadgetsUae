Frequently asked questions

Which laptop is best for work and study in UAE?
The MacBook Air M5 is the safest choice for most people: silent, fast and with all-day battery. On Windows, the ASUS Zenbook A14 and Lenovo Yoga Slim 7i are the ones we recommend.
Are laptop warranties valid in UAE?
Laptops sold by Amazon.ae normally include a manufacturer warranty valid in the UAE. The warranty length and whether it is international is listed on each product page.
Kindle or Kobo for reading in the UAE?
Kindle if you buy from the Amazon store; the Paperwhite is the best all-rounder. Kobo Clara Colour is a good pick if you read library or EPUB books.
