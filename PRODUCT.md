# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People living in the United Arab Emirates (expats and Emiratis), mostly on their phone, in English. They arrive from Google or from an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) after searching "best X in UAE" or a specific new product (iPhone 18 Pro Max, GTA 6, Zelda), and want a quick, trustworthy recommendation before buying on Amazon.ae.

## Product Purpose
GadgetsUAE (gadgetsdxb.com) is an independent Amazon Associates site that hand-picks the tech worth buying on Amazon.ae. It earns a commission when a visitor clicks through to Amazon.ae and buys (affiliate tag `gadgetsdxb-21`; a click is enough to start the referral window). Success = people find it in search/AI answers and click "View on Amazon.ae".

## Positioning
A small, opinionated, UAE-specific shortlist instead of an endless feed: one clear pick per need, written for local realities (UAE warranty, three-pin plug, heat and dust, flights out of DXB), with a pick of the day and spotlights that rotate daily for every visitor.

## Operating Context
- Static site on GitHub Pages; vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, pages generated by Node scripts (`npm run build`).
- Catalog of ~190 products in `assets/data/catalog-items.js` + `assets/js/products.js`; buying guides and category copy in `scripts/site-content.js`.
- A weekly cloud routine adds trending products; a nightly action rebuilds the JSON feed (also consumed by the Nibango app).
- Seasonal moments: White Friday / Black Friday (late November), Ramadan, back to school, summer travel.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **No prices anywhere** until a live Amazon API feed exists (Amazon policy). No "% off" or invented discounts.
- Product photos only from manufacturers' official sources (never Amazon images); some products have no photo and render a typographic card.
- All Amazon links carry the affiliate tag and `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`.
- Content in English; the owner communicates in Spanish.
- Must stay fast and work well at phone width.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: GadgetsUAE, domain gadgetsdxb.com.
- Voice: honest, direct, no hype ("No fake discounts and no clutter").
- Visual direction is open (owner: "libre"); the owner has disliked redesigns that were only colour swaps — a redesign must change structure, type and composition.

## Evidence on Hand
- Official product photos in `assets/images/<id>/` with `SOURCE.txt`.
- Ten buying guides with FAQs; daily rotation; visitor stats via Umami (no on-site numbers to show).
- No testimonials, reviews, ratings, sales numbers or press — never fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. The click to Amazon.ae is the conversion: it must be obvious on every product.
2. Opinion over inventory: fewer, clearer picks with a reason.
3. UAE-specific truth beats generic tech copy.
4. Fresh every day without lying about deals.
5. Readable by humans and by AI assistants alike (clear answers, structured data).
