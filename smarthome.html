Frequently asked questions

Do smart home devices work with UAE electrical systems?
Yes. Devices sold on Amazon.ae are rated for 220–240 V and come with the UK-style three-pin plug used in the UAE. Check the voltage only when importing from elsewhere.
Which robot vacuum is best for Dubai apartments?
The Xiaomi X20 Pro and Eufy X10 Pro Omni are excellent on tile and marble and both mop. The Roborock Qrevo Curv is the premium pick if you have thick rugs or thresholds.
Do I need an air purifier in the UAE?
On dusty and humid days a HEPA purifier makes a noticeable difference indoors, especially in bedrooms. The Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier 4 is the value choice; Dyson combines purifying with a fan.
