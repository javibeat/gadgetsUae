Frequently asked questions

Do smart home devices work with UAE electrical systems?
Yes. Devices sold on Amazon.ae are rated for 220–240 V and come with the UK-style three-pin plug used in the UAE. Check the voltage only when importing from elsewhere.
Which robot vacuum is best for Dubai apartments?
The Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 and Eufy Omni S2 are excellent on tile and marble and both mop. The Roborock Qrevo Curv 2 Pro is the premium pick if you have thick rugs or thresholds.
Do I need an air purifier in the UAE?
On dusty and humid days a HEPA purifier makes a noticeable difference indoors, especially in bedrooms. The Xiaomi Air Purifier 6 is the value choice; the Dyson HushJet Compact is the quiet bedroom pick.
