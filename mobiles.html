Frequently asked questions

Are smartphones on Amazon.ae UAE versions?
Most phones sold by Amazon.ae are official UAE or Middle East versions with local warranty. Check the listing for "UAE Version" or "Middle East Version" to be sure of network bands and warranty.
Can I trade in my old phone on Amazon.ae?
Yes, Amazon.ae runs a trade-in programme for selected devices that gives you credit towards a new phone. Eligibility is shown on the product page.
Which iPhone is the best value in 2026?
The iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 16e give you most of the current experience for less. If you want the newest camera and the biggest battery, the iPhone 18 Pro Max (released September 2026) is the one to get. See our guide to which iPhone to buy in the UAE.
Is the iPhone 18 Pro Max available in the UAE?
Yes. The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max went on sale in the UAE on 18 September 2026, and the foldable iPhone Duo followed on 23 October 2026. The standard iPhone 18 is expected in spring 2027.
