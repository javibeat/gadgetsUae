Frequently asked questions

Can I take a power bank on flights from Dubai?
Yes, in hand luggage only. Airlines cap power banks at 100 Wh (about 27,000 mAh) without approval, so every model listed here is allowed.
What charger do I need for a MacBook and a phone?
A 65 W GaN charger covers a MacBook Air and a phone at the same time. For a MacBook Pro plus phone plus earbuds, step up to a 100 W multi-port charger.
Do UK plugs work in the UAE?
Yes, the UAE uses the same three-pin Type G socket as the UK, so chargers sold on Amazon.ae plug straight in.
